Divefolio Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Data handling

Divefolio does not sell or share personal data. It has no account, no server of its own, and no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Dive records, photos, certifications, gear, and your diver profile are stored locally on your device. You can export them as a portable backup. Exported files use Apple's system share and document interfaces, and you control where those files are saved or sent.

Deleting the app deletes the logbook stored on your device. Export a backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

Health data

Divefolio can read dives recorded by Apple Watch through Apple's Health app, if you turn that on in Settings. It reads underwater depth, water temperature, diving workouts, and the workout route only, and only to create dive records on your device.

Divefolio never writes to Health, and has no server to send health data to. You can revoke access at any time in Health under Sharing, or turn the connection off in Divefolio.

Maps

Apple Maps receives a dive's entry coordinates in three situations:

  • to draw the map when you open a dive, a dive site, or the Sites map;
  • to suggest a site name for a dive with an entry position and no name, when Apple Watch brings it in and when you finish its details;
  • when you look up or pin a place in the location picker.

For a dive imported from Apple Watch, that coordinate comes from the workout route. Divefolio sends coordinates nowhere else, and a dive without coordinates produces no such request.

Location

Divefolio asks for your device's location only when you tap the button to pin a dive's entry point where you are. The position is saved with that dive on your device.

Photos

Photos you add to a dive are processed and stored locally on your device. Divefolio strips source metadata, including location, when it makes its private copy. Photos are never transmitted anywhere except when you explicitly export or share a Divefolio backup. Removing a Divefolio photo never deletes the original in your Photos library, and removing the original in Photos does not affect Divefolio's stored copy.

Saving a dive or statistics card to Photos asks only for permission to add images, never to read your library.

Backups

A .divefolio backup contains everything Divefolio stores, including your diver profile with any medical notes, emergency contacts, and insurance details you entered. Keep backups somewhere private.

Safety

Divefolio is not a dive computer, decompression planner, or source of safe-to-dive recommendations. Its gas calculators are reference values from standard training formulas, not dive planning.

Changes

This policy was updated for the release that names dive sites from the map and saves cards to Photos, described above. It will be updated again before a release introduces cloud sync, analytics, diagnostics collection, or another external service.
