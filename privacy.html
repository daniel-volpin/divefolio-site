Divefolio Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Data handling

Divefolio does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It has no account, no server of its own, and no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Dive records are created by the user and stored locally on the user's device. The user can export those records as a portable backup. Exported files use Apple's system share and document interfaces, and the user controls where those files are saved or sent.

Deleting the app may delete locally stored dive records. Export a backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

Photos

Photos the diver adds to a dive are processed and stored locally on the device. Divefolio strips source metadata when it makes its private copy. Photos are never transmitted anywhere except when the diver explicitly exports or shares a Divefolio backup. Removing a Divefolio photo never deletes the original in the Photos library, and removing the original in Photos does not affect Divefolio's stored copy.

Health data

Divefolio can read dives recorded by Apple Watch through Apple's Health app, if you turn that on in Settings. It reads underwater depth, water temperature, diving workouts, and the workout route only, and only to create dive records on your device.

Divefolio never writes to Health, and has no server to send health data to. You can revoke access at any time in Health under Sharing, or turn the connection off in Divefolio.

Maps

One thing does leave the device, and it is worth stating plainly. When a dive has an entry location and you open that dive, Apple Maps receives those coordinates in order to draw the map, the same way it does for any app that shows a map. For a dive imported from Apple Watch, that coordinate comes from the workout route.

Divefolio sends those coordinates nowhere else. A dive without coordinates shows no map and produces no such request.

Safety

Divefolio is not a dive computer, decompression planner, or source of safe-to-dive recommendations.

Changes

This policy was updated for the release that introduced photos, described above. It will be updated again before a release introduces cloud sync, analytics, diagnostics collection, or another external service.
