Support

Where is my data stored?

Dive records are stored locally on the device. Divefolio does not currently provide an account or cloud sync.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings → Back up Divefolio and save the .divefolio package somewhere you control, such as iCloud Drive. It contains every dive and photo.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings → Restore backup, choose a .divefolio package, or a legacy .json archive from before photos existed, and confirm the import. Matching records are updated and other records are added.

Can Divefolio replace my dive computer?

No. Divefolio does not calculate decompression limits, no-fly intervals, residual nitrogen, or safe-to-dive recommendations.
